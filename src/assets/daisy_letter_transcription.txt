Pawford, 12 April 2006

Dear Luna,

I found another envelope of family photographs while clearing the study. A few have names on the back, but most do not. I am putting them in order before the details slip from memory.

The photograph of Silver beside the garden gate was taken shortly after his birthday. Barnaby says the gate was painted that spring. I will write his recollection next to the print, rather than on it.

When you visit, perhaps we can compare the pictures with the register extracts and add the missing names together. Please bring the small album you mentioned.

With love,
Daisy
